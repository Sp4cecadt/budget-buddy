# Budget

A simple payday-to-payday budget you can add to an iPhone Home Screen. It lives on the phone only — nothing is sent anywhere.

**Open it:** https://sp4cecadt.github.io/budget-buddy/

## Add to Home Screen

1. Open the site in Safari on iPhone.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. The Budget icon will sit with your other apps and work offline.

## What it does

- Set a weekly or fortnightly amount and your payday.
- Each day shows how much you can spend (money left ÷ days left, including today).
- Add spends with a big keypad. Unusual amounts are checked before they save.
- A gentle heads-up if a spend uses most of, or more than, today’s amount.
- On payday, start a fresh period. Leftover money does not carry over unless you choose that.

## Privacy

All data stays in this browser on this phone (`localStorage`). There is no account and no server.
